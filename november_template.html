Data basis

Actual revenue is pulled live from the ERP ledger (GL 3010001, 3010002, 3010005, 3010006, net of returns) for the period 1–__ASOF__, at build time. Two shipping entities post in foreign currency and are converted to BDT: ASLL-1 / PTE (USD ×116.127) and ASeLLC Ship Management (AED ×32.798).

Budget and Forecast are the full-month November targets from the official sub-schedule workbook, so achievement is month-to-date measured against a full-month plan.

G2G (Bongo & MTS & Shipping BADC/BCIC) maps to ERP SBU 77, Daily Trading Company Ltd. This was verified against the file and the system: the sheet’s 15 Nov G2G value (10.5804 Cr) sits exactly on ERP SBU 77 for 1–15 Nov, and the November review deck’s to-8-Nov figure (7.04 Cr) matches at 7.0398 Cr. A later sheet revision showed 67.9354 Cr, but ERP SBU 77 is only 11.7453 Cr at 17 Nov with zero postings that day, so the report uses the ledger figure.
